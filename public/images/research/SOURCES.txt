colbert-controls.svg
Selected conditions redrawn from Figure 1(a) of "Exact but Not Specific: A Measurement-Validity Analysis of Token-Level Name-Swap Audits in Late-Interaction Retrieval" (research manuscript, Ziyu Kong).
Uses corrected-scoring estimates and both test-level and swap-pair cluster-bootstrap 95% intervals. Each displayed condition has 280 tests and five swap pairs; the numeral condition uses modified templates. This is a selected control panel, not evidence of equivalence.

rl-probe-control.svg
Redrawn from Figure 2(a) of "What Survives a Layout Shift? Diagnosing Retention in Continual Navigation" (research manuscript, Junhe Chen and Ziyu Kong, equal contribution).
Layout-A linear decoding, homeostatic-reward condition. All 37 seed observations, original quasi-trimmed means, and 95% percentile-bootstrap intervals are retained. Episode counts are matched; interactions and updates are not.
