rl-method.svg
Figure 1 of "What Survives a Layout Shift? Diagnosing Retention in Continual Navigation" (research manuscript, Junhe Chen and Ziyu Kong, equal contribution).
Converted from the manuscript's new_evidence/overview.pdf to SVG without changing its panels, labels, colors, or layout. Original PDF SHA256: be92987cd1e31867f60937fa93183efa265a628c7ff4509b94c1136c9a8494e4.
The diagram presents changing resource layouts and the separate diagnostic controls for action sensitivity, decodable geometry, and behavioral retention. It is an evaluation overview, not a new anti-forgetting algorithm.

colbert-method.svg
New method schematic based on section 3 of "Exact but Not Specific: A Measurement-Validity Analysis of Token-Level Name-Swap Audits in Late-Interaction Retrieval" (research manuscript, Ziyu Kong).
Shows matched document perturbations, the checkpoint-projection and L2-normalized MaxSim scoring path, signed token attribution, and non-identity specificity controls. The exact score decomposition includes all query positions, including special tokens. Token matches are schematic, with no measured scores or result ratios.
This is an adaptation of the current manuscript's methods, not an original numbered paper figure. The older overview figure's result ratios are not reused.

colbert-controls.svg
Selected conditions redrawn from Figure 1(a) of "Exact but Not Specific: A Measurement-Validity Analysis of Token-Level Name-Swap Audits in Late-Interaction Retrieval" (research manuscript, Ziyu Kong).
Uses corrected-scoring estimates and both test-level and swap-pair cluster-bootstrap 95% intervals. Each displayed condition has 280 tests and five swap pairs; the numeral condition uses modified templates. This is a selected control panel, not evidence of equivalence.

rl-probe-control.svg
Redrawn from Figure 2(a) of "What Survives a Layout Shift? Diagnosing Retention in Continual Navigation" (research manuscript, Junhe Chen and Ziyu Kong, equal contribution).
Layout-A linear decoding, homeostatic-reward condition. All 37 seed observations, original quasi-trimmed means, and 95% percentile-bootstrap intervals are retained. Episode counts are matched; interactions and updates are not.
